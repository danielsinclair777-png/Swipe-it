A CONCEPT WITH POTENTIAL

Meet Swipe-it.

A standalone, swipe-first organiser for your Reddit collections.

● FUNCTIONAL FRONTEND PROTOTYPE

Built to show the idea, not pretend it's connected.

This early version demonstrates the interface, swipe gestures, category filters, gallery, session statistics, undo, and browser-local persistence using fictional sample content.

What works today

Swipe left/right or use buttons; browse sample collections; filter by media type; undo your last decision; inspect a gallery; reset your progress. Progress is stored on your device using localStorage.

What comes later

Reddit OAuth authentication, real account-history retrieval, supported account-management actions, proper media previews, and secure production deployment. Those features require additional development and appropriate Reddit API access.

Privacy

No login, analytics, external APIs, or third-party JavaScript. The included illustrations are CSS-generated placeholders, not Reddit media. All demo state stays in this browser.
